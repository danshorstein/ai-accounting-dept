# Riverton Sporting Goods, Inc. — Company Profile

Riverton Sporting Goods, Inc. is a mid-sized U.S. sporting-goods distributor.

The company purchases sporting goods from manufacturers and sells them to retailers and direct customers.

## Current accounting scope

The following accounting concepts apply:

- General ledger
- Operating cash
- Accounts payable
- Accounts receivable
- Month-end close
- Policies and procedures

The company uses a calendar fiscal year.
