# Independent Review Control — Bank Reconciliations

## Control objective

Detect errors, unsupported reconciling items, inappropriate assumptions, incomplete investigation, or noncompliance with the Cash Reconciliation Policy before the reconciliation is considered complete.

## Control design

A reviewer who did not prepare the reconciliation reviews the completed workpaper and supporting evidence.

The review should consider:

- whether the correct bank account and GL account were used;
- whether the source data appears complete;
- whether transaction matches are reasonable;
- whether reconciling items are appropriately classified and supported;
- whether unusual or material exceptions were investigated;
- whether proposed adjustments are supported;
- whether unresolved items are clearly disclosed;
- whether the reconciliation complies with company policy.

The reviewer should either:

- approve the reconciliation; or
- return it to the preparer with questions or required corrections.

## Evidence

Evidence of the review should be retained with the reconciliation workpaper.

For the first experiment, the human supervising Claude will perform the independent review.
