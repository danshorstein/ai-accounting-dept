# Finance & Accounting Organization

Riverton Sporting Goods has a traditional and simple corporate accounting structure for a small business.

## Current organization

Controller (the human, currently)  
  └── Staff Accountant

Additional accounting roles may be added later as the experiment grows, such as:

- Accounts Payable Specialist
- Accounts Receivable Specialist
- Internal Audit
- Financial Planning & Analysis
- Senior Accountnat
- CFO

For the first experiment, the only role we are focused on is the **Staff Accountant**.

## Staff Accountant context

The Staff Accountant is expected to perform routine accounting work, prepare reconciliations and workpapers, investigate exceptions, and escalate issues when they require higher-level judgment.

The Staff Accountant does not independently approve their own work.
