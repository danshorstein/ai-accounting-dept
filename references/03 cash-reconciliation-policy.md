# Cash Reconciliation Policy

## Purpose

To ensure cash balances recorded in the general ledger are supported by external bank evidence and that reconciling items are properly identified, investigated, supported, and reviewed.

## Policy requirements

1. All operating bank accounts must be reconciled monthly.

2. The reconciliation must compare bank activity and balances to the related general-ledger cash account.

3. Reconciling items must be identified and supported by available evidence.

4. Unsupported differences must not be cleared, plugged, or otherwise forced solely to make the reconciliation balance.

5. Material or unusual reconciling items must be investigated and escalated when appropriate.

6. The reconciliation should clearly distinguish between:
   - timing differences;
   - items requiring a general-ledger adjustment;
   - items requiring bank investigation;
   - unresolved exceptions.

7. Reconciliation preparation and review should be performed by different individuals.

8. Evidence supporting the reconciliation and its review must be retained.
