# Riverton Sporting Goods, Inc. — Company Profile

Riverton Sporting Goods, Inc. is a fictional mid-sized U.S. sporting-goods distributor used for this AI accounting department experiment.

The company purchases sporting goods from manufacturers and sells them to retailers and direct customers.

## Current accounting scope

For the first experiment, only a small portion of the accounting environment matters:

- General ledger
- Operating cash
- Accounts payable
- Accounts receivable
- Month-end close

The company uses a calendar fiscal year.

## Purpose of the experiment

This repository is being used to explore how AI agents can be trained to perform roles within a corporate Finance & Accounting department.

The environment should be treated like a real company, but all data and entities are fictional.
